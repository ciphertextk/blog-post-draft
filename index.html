Why Platform Income Is Invisible to Lenders Today

The gig economy has fundamentally changed how people earn money. Uber drivers, Shopify merchants, YouTube creators, TikTok Shop sellers, and Stripe-connected businesses now represent hundreds of millions of workers globally — yet the lending infrastructure built to serve them has barely changed since the era of the paper pay stub.

The core problem is documentation. A traditional underwriter needs verifiable proof of income: something they can check, authenticate, and trust. Salaried employees get this automatically through payroll systems. Platform earners get nothing that works inside a loan application.

The workarounds lenders use today — bank statement analysis, tax return review, manual document uploads — are slow, expensive, and miss the signal entirely. A Shopify merchant who runs most revenue through the platform won't have meaningful payroll records. An Uber driver with strong monthly earnings might show a fraction of that on a tax return after deductions. The income is real. The verification system is broken everywhere.

Manual document review pushes underwriting timelines to weeks. False negatives are high. And gig workers, e-commerce merchants, and creators get systematically excluded — not because they can't repay, but because the verification layer doesn't exist for them.

What Cr3dentials Does

Cr3dentials is income verification infrastructure for digital lenders. Lenders connect via a single API. Borrowers verify their platform income — from Uber, Shopify, YouTube, Stripe, TikTok Shop, and more — directly inside the lender's existing application, in seconds, without uploading documents and without Cr3dentials or the lender ever seeing the raw underlying data.

The verification uses ZK-TLS (zero-knowledge Transport Layer Security) — a cryptographic protocol that proves a claim is true without revealing the data behind it. Instead of sharing an Uber earnings statement, a borrower can prove "my Uber income exceeds $3,000/month over the past 6 months" — and a lender can cryptographically verify that claim in real time.

Key distinction: Cr3dentials does not require platform API partnerships to operate. Because we use ZK-TLS, any web platform a borrower can log into can be verified — no agreements with Shopify, Uber, or YouTube needed. This is fundamentally different from bank data aggregators or open banking solutions that depend on platform cooperation.

Verification records are anchored onchain via Ethereum Attestation Service (EAS), making them portable across capital partners. A borrower who verifies their income with one lender can reuse that attestation with another — without going through the process again.

How It Works: ZK-TLS in Plain English

Zero-knowledge proofs can sound intimidating. Here is the simple version of what happens when a borrower uses Cr3dentials inside a lender's application:

1

Borrower verifies income inside the lender's existing flow

The entire verification happens within the lender's own product — no redirects, no new accounts, no third-party portals. From the borrower's perspective, it feels completely native.

2

Borrower connects their earning platform

They authenticate with Uber, Shopify, YouTube, Stripe, or any other supported platform the same way they'd normally log in. Their credentials go directly to the platform. Cr3dentials never sees or stores them.

3

ZK-TLS cryptographically attests to the earnings

The protocol generates a zero-knowledge proof of the relevant income claims — amount, time period, consistency — without transmitting the underlying raw data anywhere. The mathematics of the proof make it impossible to forge.

4

Lender receives a verified, structured decision signal

Within seconds, the lender's system receives a cryptographically verified response — income confirmed, identity matched, platform reputation scored. No document review, no human in the loop, no waiting.

5

Attestation is portable across capital partners

Because records are anchored via Ethereum Attestation Service, the borrower owns their verification. It can be reused with any other lender, DeFi protocol, or capital provider — without repeating the process.

"ZK banking allows a borrower to prove they have a specific credit score or valid proof of income without uploading their bank statements onchain. A lender can verify the risk profile and issue a loan based on cryptographic assurance rather than public exposure."— Chainlink, ZK Banking Research

Frequently Asked Questions

How can a lender verify Uber driver income without Uber's API?

Cr3dentials uses ZK-TLS — a cryptographic protocol that works at the TLS (HTTPS) layer. When a borrower connects their Uber driver account through Cr3dentials' verification flow, the protocol captures the authenticated session and generates a zero-knowledge proof of their earnings data. No Uber API partnership is required. The proof is delivered to the lender; the raw earnings data never leaves the borrower's session.

What is the difference between Cr3dentials and open banking / Plaid?

Open banking solutions like Plaid connect to bank accounts and require bank partnerships. They show cash deposits — but for platform earners, those deposits are commingled with personal spending and don't reflect platform reputation or earnings consistency. Cr3dentials verifies income directly at the source platform using cryptographic proofs, with no raw data exposure to the lender and no requirement for platform API agreements.

Does Cr3dentials store borrower data?

No. Cr3dentials never stores or transmits raw borrower data. The ZK-TLS protocol generates a cryptographic proof that contains only the verified claim — for example, "monthly Uber earnings exceed $X" — without any underlying personal data, login credentials, or transaction history. What is anchored onchain via Ethereum Attestation Service is the proof itself, not the source data.

How does Cr3dentials handle lenders who work across multiple countries?

Because attestations are anchored via Ethereum Attestation Service, they are globally portable and not tied to any jurisdiction's banking infrastructure. A lender operating across South Africa, Nigeria, and the US — or a DeFi protocol open to global borrowers — can accept the same cryptographic proof regardless of geography. Active markets include South Africa and Nigeria, with pipeline covering Kenya, the US, Hong Kong, and LATAM.

How does Cr3dentials integrate into an existing lending workflow?

Cr3dentials connects to a lender's existing workflow via a simple API integration. The borrower verification experience is fully contained within the lender's own product — no redirects, no third-party portals, no new accounts for the borrower to create. Setup takes hours, not months. Two pricing models: per-verification pricing for high-throughput consumer lenders, and revenue share for high-loan-size commercial lenders.

What is Ethereum Attestation Service and why does Cr3dentials use it?

Ethereum Attestation Service (EAS) is a public, open protocol for creating and storing verifiable attestations onchain. Cr3dentials anchors every verification to EAS so the proof is permanent, tamper-proof, and portable. A borrower who verifies their Shopify income once can reuse that attestation with any other lender, capital partner, or DeFi protocol that recognizes EAS-anchored records.
